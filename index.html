Case Study & System Blueprint

AgriMitra: Next-Gen Agricultural Advisory & Agronomic Platform

Client Context: National Agricultural Transformation & AgriStack (Digital Public Infrastructure)

India's agrarian economy sustains over 55% of the national workforce across 15 distinct agro-climatic zones. However, smallholder and marginal farmers face acute productivity bottlenecks: regional microclimate divergence, sudden pest outbreaks costing ₹1.4 lakh crore annually, delayed diagnostic advice, and fragmented access to welfare schemes. AgriMitra was engineered as a comprehensive digital agriculture intelligence engine delivering precision agronomic recommendations, diagnostic solvers, and AI-driven advisory across every Indian state.

🎯 Core Objective

To democratize expert agronomic intelligence for every smallholder farmer in India by combining localized soil profiles, seasonal crop requirements, instant symptom-based pest diagnosis, and government subsidy access into an intuitive, zero-barrier platform.

⚙️ Architectural Pillars

  • Pan-India Coverage: 28 States + 8 UTs with soils, rainfall, districts, and advisories.
  • Multi-Intent AI Engine: Answers questions on crops, fertilizers, weather, and schemes.
  • Dual Treatment Solver: Provides precision chemical dosage alongside eco-friendly organic remedies.
  • Multi-Runtime Backend: Powered by native PowerShell, Python, or Node.js with zero dependencies.
